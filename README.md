Some of the work I did at Triton Neurotech (a club at UCSD). 
The goal of the project I was a part of was to use signals from a Unicorn Hybrid Black headset to help users move the wheelchair with just their intentions.
The work I did was focused on building a classifier for electrical signals recorded by the different channels on the headsets by first trying to build the classifier  off of public data. 
We later decided that a blink detection system for EEG based input would have far more accuracy, simplicity and reliability and decided to work towards that.
